\section{Competency Questions}
Data: UK DfT STATS19 2025, all collisions of North Wales Police and Gwent Police. ``Serious or fatal'' means KSI (killed or seriously injured).
\small
\begin{longtable}{L{0.9cm} L{6.0cm} L{2.1cm} L{6.6cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Competency question} & \textbf{Category} & \textbf{Why it needs the KG}\\
\midrule
\endhead
CQ1 & Which collisions involved a vulnerable road user (pedestrian, cyclist or motorcyclist), and what type of vehicle was it? & Multi-hop & Three hops (collision, vehicle, casualty); \cls{Pedestrian}, \cls{Cyclist} and \cls{Motorcyclist} roll up to \cls{VulnerableRoadUser}. The answer is a list of collision IDs.\\
CQ2 & Which five local authorities had the most serious or fatal collisions on A roads in wet or icy conditions? & Aggregation & Exact counts under four combined filters for one year and one sample.\\
CQ3 & Which areas are high-risk, and which police force covers each one? & Inference & \cls{HighRiskArea} is derived by a counting rule; the area chain is transitive. Needs counts per small area.\\
CQ4 & When a pedestrian was killed or seriously hurt, what was the vehicle doing, and was it dark? & Multi-hop & Casualty to vehicle to collision; \cls{Pedestrian} and KSI come from class hierarchies, light is read from the collision.\\
CQ5 & In each police force area, how many serious or fatal collisions happened in the dark and how many in daylight? & Aggregation & Four darkness labels are grouped as ``dark'' before counting, per police force reached through the area hierarchy.\\
CQ6 & Which serious or fatal collisions happened in the Gwent police area, and which of them involved a vulnerable road user? & Inference & No collision is stored in a police force area or as serious, fatal or vulnerable-road-user: all three come from the transitive area chain and the defined classes \cls{KSICollision} and \cls{VRUCollision}.\\
CQ7 & When a cyclist was killed or seriously hurt, what other vehicles were involved? & Multi-hop & Casualty, own vehicle, collision, other vehicles: four hops and a count.\\
CQ8 & Which type of road has the most serious or fatal collisions? & Aggregation & Grouping and counting by road type (optionally by force through the area hierarchy).\\
CQ9 & Which children were killed or seriously hurt, and were they pedestrians, passengers or drivers? & Inference & \cls{ChildCasualty} is inferred by rule from age bands 1 to 3; the casualty links back to its collision.\\
CQ10 & Which collisions on fast roads (50\,mph or more) left a motorcyclist killed or seriously hurt? & Multi-hop & FILTER on the collision, inference on casualty type two hops away.\\
CQ11 & At which type of junction do most serious or fatal collisions happen? & Aggregation & Counting by junction type; an optional extra adds pedestrian counts with OPTIONAL.\\
CQ12 & For a given collision identifier, what are its associated vehicles, casualties, severity, road and weather conditions, and administrative areas (LSOA, local authority and police force)? & LLM-hard & Record-level retrieval across every hop; an LLM cannot know or list real collision records.\\
CQ13 & In each high-risk area, how many serious or fatal collisions involved a vulnerable road user? & LLM-hard & Combines a derived class with exact counts per area; no outside source holds this answer.\\
CQ14 & Which collisions are most similar to this fatal collision? & KG reasoning & Answered by the KG reasoning component (TransE or multi-hop similarity) in a later iteration.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section{CQ-to-KG Mapping}
Names are those of the ontology file \texttt{crash\_schema.ttl}. Arrows show the graph path; $^{+}$ is the SPARQL property-path operator. ``KSI'' = \cls{KSICollision} (R1) or, for a casualty, \textit{casualtySeverity} is ``Fatal'' or ``Serious''. Road type, junction, weather, light and surface are text values (data properties), so they are matched with FILTER.

\normalsize
\noindent\textbf{Rules.} R1 KSICollision = FatalCollision or SeriousCollision. R3 VRUCollision = collision with involvesCasualty some VulnerableRoadUser. R4 Pedestrian, Cyclist, Motorcyclist are subclasses of VulnerableRoadUser. R5 partOf is transitive (LSOA, local authority, police force). R6 involvesCasualty = hasVehicle then hasCasualty. R1 to R6 are OWL2-RL; R7 HighRiskArea (LSOA with at least 3 KSICollisions) and R8 ChildCasualty (age band 1 to 3) are SPARQL INSERT queries because GraphDB has no SWRL.
